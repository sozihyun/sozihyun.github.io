Last summer in Halifax was made unforgettable by many afternoons spent paddling along the Northwest Arm and the harbour. Gliding past the Halifax skyline on calm water, with seabirds overhead and the ocean breeze in my face, was the perfect way to unwind.

Kayaking here is easy to pick up: rentals are available right by the waterfront, and even a beginner can enjoy a peaceful loop around the harbour. I would paddle out at golden hour, when the light turned the water silver and the city looked its best from the sea.

If you ever visit Halifax in the summer, do not miss the chance to get on the water.
